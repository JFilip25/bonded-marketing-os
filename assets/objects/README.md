# Cutout objects

All objects are AI-generated household objects with no people and no logos, as the campaign's image style asks. They were made with Canva AI image generation in the owner's Canva account.

**How each file was made:**
1. Each image was generated on a near-Paper background with a soft contact shadow.
2. It was exported at its native 1264×1264 from a working Canva page (the 9 Oct pair at 1080×1080).
3. `tools/objects/blend-to-paper.py` flattened the background to exact Paper `#F5F2EA` and cropped to the object.

**Handwriting** on the calendars, lists, tags and notepads is loose, illegible scribble by design. No readable words were generated. Calendar date numbers are the only legible characters.

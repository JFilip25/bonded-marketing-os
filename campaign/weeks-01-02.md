# Bonded: 3-month waitlist campaign, weeks 1 and 2

October 2026

## Campaign overview

Bonded runs a 3-month waitlist campaign on Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, posting every day. This file covers weeks 1 and 2 in full: a pinned introduction, 14 feed posts and 14 days of stories.

**Goal:** waitlist signups on the Bonded website. Every post ends with Bonded and the waitlist, and everything before it has to be worth reading on its own. The account has to be worth following first.

**The 3 months**

| Weeks | Phase | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 to 4 | Name the problem | The mental load, everyday family life, what Bonded is. Build the audience. |
| 5 to 8 | Show how it works | Product walkthroughs, practical tips, the holiday season. |
| 9 to 12 | The family reset | New Year reset, waitlist push, launch countdown once there is a real date. |

**Weeks 1 and 2.** Week 1 is "It's all in my head": the mental load, and meeting Bonded. Week 2 is "Fair, calm and together": how Bonded works in a real week.

**Start date.** Day 1 is a Monday, once the waitlist page is live. Starting Monday 19 October puts week 12 on 4 to 10 January, right on the New Year reset.

**What changed.** This replaces the stealth, Instagram-only plan in the content engine doc. It is now public, daily, on three platforms, with a website waitlist. Positioning, pillars and claims rules stay the same.

## Audience and messages

**Who we're talking to:** parents of children aged 4 to 18 who carry the household plan in their head and want calmer weeks and more time together. Mostly mothers aged 30 to 45, plus fathers who like a system.

**Brand line:** Small contributions. More time together. It lives in the bio and on the waitlist page, not at the end of every post.

| Pillar | The family moment behind it | Where Bonded fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One shared plan | Being the only person who knows what's happening this week | Shared plan and responsibilities |
| Effort gets noticed | Jobs nobody notices because nothing went wrong | Approvals with a note, points |
| Money they understand | An allowance nobody ever properly agreed on | Allowance and earnings tracking, savings goals |
| Something to look forward to | Weekend plans that stay ideas | Family experiences everyone works toward |
| Kids have a say | Kids who skip a job instead of asking to move it | Help, break and reschedule requests |

**Funnel stage.** Weeks 1 and 2 are mostly awareness. Every post gives parents something useful first. Bonded comes once, at the end, with the waitlist.

## Voice

**The test for every post:** take Bonded out of it. If nothing worth reading, saving or sharing is left, the post gets rewritten.

**Who's talking.** An observant, occasionally funny parent-creator. First person for observations about family life ("Apparently, I'm the family calendar"), "we" when talking about building Bonded. It never claims a specific event happened to a real family, and never invents a testimonial.

**How it sounds.** Specific situations instead of parenting concepts. Plain sentences with natural rhythm. A clear point of view. Funny when something is funny, not because a post needs a joke. No reassurance about doing your best, no inspirational endings. Captions are as long as the idea needs, from one line to a few paragraphs, and each platform gets its own.

**How Bonded shows up**

- **Every post** (D-2026-10-15-1 and D-2026-10-15-2): the parent's problem, the feeling, what to do, then Bonded and the waitlist on the last slide and at the end of the captions. No feature talk: the one line about Bonded is the positioning in plain words.

**Never write:** seamless, effortlessly, game-changing, unlock, transform, streamline, empower, say goodbye to chaos, making memories, your parenting journey, or slogan pairs like "Less stress. More memories." No POV framing, "plot twist", empty "again", emoji, hashtag clouds or em dashes.

**Never claim** that rewards change children's behaviour, that every job should be paid, or any result Bonded can't promise.

## Slide style

**Since Amendments 2 and 3,** every feed post in weeks 1 and 2 uses the messaging v2 look instead: full photo backgrounds, Paper type on an Ink gradient, and the plain waitlist slide (D-2026-10-15-4, `proposals/messaging-v2.md`). This section stays as written for reference.

The look follows your examples: editorial serif on a light grey page, lots of space, one idea per slide. It applies to every carousel and single image post.

**Palette**

- Background: light warm grey #E8E6E2.
- Text: near black #151515. Contrast is above 15:1, so it reads on dim screens.
- Pills and small labels: mid grey #6E6E6E with white text.
- Accent: deep green #0F6B5C, on one word or one diagram element per slide at most. Swap in the Bonded brand colour if you have one.

**Type**

- Headlines: Playfair Display. Regular for the setup word, Black Italic for the payoff word, stacked and slightly overlapping, like "Ugly / Carousel" in your example.
- Subheadlines and small text: Poppins Medium.
- Sizes on 1080 × 1440: headline 120 to 190px, subheadline 44px, small text 28px, handle 26px.

**Images.** Cutout objects (a sock, a plate, a coin jar, a kitchen timer) with a soft contact shadow, so they sit on the page instead of looking pasted flat. AI-generated, no people. No emoji: a small cutout does the job the emoji does in the examples.

**Reading order.** Big headline first, subheadline second, small text last. Top to bottom, left to right.

**Carousel structure,** from your framework slide: slide 1 hooks and every slide after it earns the swipe. Use a diagram where it explains something faster than words. Pick the structure the idea needs. The last slide finishes the thought, and carries a CTA only when the post mentions Bonded.

**The five layouts** (positions measured from the top of the 1080 × 1440 canvas)

- **A. Versus cover.** For slide 1 and single images that compare two things. Handle top centre at 200px. Top statement centred between 260 and 640px. A thin grey line across at 720px, with a grey "VS" pill 110px from the left edge and a grey "Swipe" pill 110px from the right edge. Bottom statement centred between 800 and 1180px.
- **B. Statement cover.** For slide 1 and single images with one big line. Handle top centre at 200px. Headline centred between 300 and 860px. Subheadline centred 40px below it. Cutout object centred between 960 and 1220px.
- **C. Content.** For slides 2 onward. Small grey label top left at 200px ("01", or the age band). Headline top left from 260px, 2 lines at most. Subheadline 32px below it, 3 lines at most. Cutout object bottom right, inside the margins, about 40% of the width.
- **D. Diagram.** Headline top left from 260px. Diagram centred between 620 and 1180px: numbered circles and thin arrows in black, with the accent on one element.
- **E. CTA.** For a last slide that asks for something. Most carousels finish on Layout C instead. Headline centred at 560px. Subheadline centred 40px below it. Grey pill "Link in bio" centred at 1200px.

## Raw video style

**Not used in weeks 1 and 2 since Amendment 3.** The owner's clips run as moving carousel slides instead (D-2026-10-15-7). This section stays as written for later weeks.

Real family moments, filmed on a phone. An off-camera parent voiceover tells it, the burned-in text carries the key lines for people watching on mute, and the caption adds what the video doesn't say.

- **Footage:** vertical 1080 × 1920, natural light, handheld, cuts only. 15 to 25 seconds. Kids or the whole family doing something real: setting the table, folding laundry, a Saturday reset, a bike ride.
- **Headline (burned in):** the first line, on screen from 0 to 3 seconds. Poppins SemiBold 72px, white with a soft shadow, centred, starting 320px from the top.
- **Subheadlines (burned in):** the lines that follow, one at a time, each on screen for 2 to 3 seconds. Poppins SemiBold 62px, white with a soft shadow, centred, same position as the headline. Never below 1570px and never within 150px of the right edge.
- **End card:** the last 2 seconds, only on videos that mention Bonded. The final frame dimmed to 60%, with "Bonded" in Playfair Display Regular 80px centred at 860px and "Join the waitlist" in Playfair Display Black Italic 110px centred below it.
- **Sound:** the voiceover on top, natural sound kept low underneath, and a quiet track from each platform's commercial music library where it helps.
- **Kids on camera:** only with written consent from their parents. No names, school logos, house numbers or locations in frame.
- **App on screen:** the demo family only, with "Demo family" in small text.

## CTA and stories

CTAs are a judgment call. Each brief names one, or says "No CTA".

- **Waitlist,** on every post (D-2026-10-15-2). Instagram: "Comment WAITLIST and we'll send you the link." ManyChat sends it by DM, and the link is in the bio. Facebook: "The waitlist is here: [link]". TikTok: "Waitlist link in bio", or "Waitlist link on our Instagram" if the account can't show a bio link yet.
- **Conversation,** a real question, only when the answers would be worth reading.
- **No CTA:** not used in weeks 1 and 2, since every post ends on the waitlist.

**Stories, Instagram and Facebook** (1080 × 1920, everything between 250px from the top and 250px from the bottom). Stories are where people take part, so they don't repeat the feed. Each day uses one of four types, named in each brief:

- **Idea:** one useful tip on a single frame. Playfair Display Black Italic 96px centred at 860px, one Poppins line below it.
- **Question:** a question people genuinely want to answer, with "Reply and tell us" underneath. A poll or question sticker works better here if you're happy to lift the no-stickers rule; place it centred at 1300px.
- **Share-back:** the best replies to an earlier question, shared anonymously or with permission.
- **Behind the scenes:** a screen, a sketch or a decision from building Bonded. These are the only stories that carry the waitlist link sticker, centred at 1300px.

Share the day's feed post to stories only when it adds something, not by default.

## Success metrics

Weeks 1 and 2 set the baseline. Targets for weeks 3 to 12 come from these numbers, not guesses.

| Metric | Where to find it | What it tells you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Waitlist signups by platform | Website analytics, through the tagged links | Whether the campaign works |
| WAITLIST comments and DMs sent | ManyChat | Instagram's real conversion step |
| Saves and shares per slideshow | Instagram and TikTok insights | Which topics people keep |
| Hold rate in the first 3 seconds, average watch time | Reels and TikTok insights | Which hooks work |
| Profile visits and link clicks | Each platform's insights | Whether interest turns into a visit |

**Review every Sunday, 15 minutes.** Note the three best posts and why. From week 3, make more of what worked and cut the weakest format.

**Decisions for you**

- **Link sticker in stories.** The earlier rule was no stickers. I'd make one exception: the link sticker is the only clickable link in Instagram stories.
- **A reason to join now.** Early access or a founding-family price gives people a reason to sign up today. Only offer one you will honour.
- **First market.** US, UK or EU decides posting times, spelling (allowance or pocket money) and hashtags.

**Risks**

- **Daily posting wears thin.** All 15 posts are made before Day 1. If a day slips, reshare the best post so far rather than rush a new one.
- **Kids on camera.** Written consent for every child filmed. No names, schools or locations in frame.
- **Claims run ahead of the app.** Anything not in the current build stays out, even if it's coming soon.

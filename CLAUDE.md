## Product and positioning
BONDED: **A family app for running the week together.** Tagline: **Small contributions. More time together.**
A shared family calendar and responsibilities, child check-offs, parent approvals and notes, optional points toward family-agreed extra experiences, optional money/allowance/earnings tracking, and requests for help/break/reschedule. BONDED records money; it does not move money. Never suggest everyday parental attention or family time must be earned.
Audience: parents of children approximately 4-18. Do not market directly to children.
Demo family: the Millers, Mom, Dad, Emma (8), Leo (13). Screens must use actual app screenshots, visibly labeled "Demo family". Never fabricate UI, customers, reviews or outcomes.

# BONDED designer production brief | October 2026

The exact Day-by-Day brief, captions, frames and visual coordinates live in `campaign/weeks-01-02.md`. That approved file is authoritative on all content. This file locks production order and delivery.

## Goal
Design finished assets for the Day 0 pinned intro and Days 1–14. Parent-facing, editorial magazine aesthetic, not a corporate technology campaign. Don't rewrite campaign copy. BONDED is a family app for running the week together, not merely a chore tracker.

## Strict Step 1: approval before full production
Produce and present together:
- Layout templates A, B, C, D, E at 1080 x 1440.
- Four Story templates: Idea, Question, Share-back, Behind the scenes at 1080 x 1920.
- Burned-in video text treatment and Day 11 end card at 1080 x 1920.
- **The entire seven-slide Day 2 carousel**, correctly typeset.
**STOP HERE until a human explicitly approves the design system.**

## Step 2: static graphics
After approval: Day 0 (6 slides), Day 2 (7 already approved), Day 3 (1), Day 5 (8), Day 7 (1), Day 8 (7), Day 10 (7), Day 12 (8), Day 14 (1). **46 feed frames total**, reused as platform-compatible original files where applicable.

## Step 3: Stories
Fourteen daily sets (Day 1 through Day 14), plus Day 0 About highlight cover and introductory slides 2–5 adapted to Story ratio. Story types: Question (1,3,4,8,10,13,14); Idea (2,7,9); Share-back (6,12, with unfilled genuine-reply placeholders); Behind the scenes (5,11 with space for native link sticker at vertical centre 1300px). Interactive stickers added on-platform, never fake clickable art.

## Step 4: six videos
Days 1,4,6,9,11,13 are filmed live by families, NOT generated by designer. Deliver every script's burnt-in text line as **separate transparent PNG** at 1080 x 1920, correct timecode and position. Day 11 also includes one end card with transparent region for dimmed actual footage. Do not pretend finished video is included.

## Visual rules
Use the exact campaign's sizing, safe zones and A-E layouts. 1080x1440 static graphic with 180px top/bottom and 50px sides; Reel 1080x1920 with 250px top, 350px bottom and 150px right; Stories 1080x1920 with 250px top/bottom. Keep cover text inside centre 1080x1350 crop. Warm grey #E8E6E2, near-black #151515, grey #6E6E6E and one deep green #0F6B5C emphasis at most per slide pending real BONDED brand asset reconciliation. Playfair Display Regular/Black Italic headlines, Poppins smaller copy. AI generated **cutout household objects only**, soft shadow and slight natural wear. No stock photos, no people, no AI children, no emoji.
Use **only true supplied screenshots** labeled Demo family. If unavailable place clearly marked placeholder and record blocker. Don't invent logo; use typeset "Bonded" if missing, placeholder "@[handle]" until real account known. No added words, hashtags, CTAs, prices, badges, fake ratings, or em dashes. BONDED is not a bank or payment app.

## Naming/handover
sRGB PNG exports at full size, editable Canva sources, folders `templates/` and `day00/` through `day14/`.
Names like `day02_carousel_s01.png`, `day03_single.png`, `day05_story_f1.png`, `day06_video_04s-10s.png`, `day11_endcard.png`, `day00_highlight_cover.png`.
Produce Instagram grid preview in newest-first posting order after usable real video covers exist (or label the missing positions). Report text fit, placeholder screens, authentic screenshots outstanding, any day/general-style conflict and actual exported items.

## Accuracy/QA
No payment UI, transfers, bank cards, store badges, fabricated screens, invented testimonials or performance. Points for agreed optional extras only; ordinary time and attention must never be conditional. If text won't fit, scale only within approved range and flag unresolved overflow. Verify actual capabilities of Canva MCP for transparent PNG export and templates rather than claiming it did so.

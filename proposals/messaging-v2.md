# Messaging v2: the problem, the feeling, what to do, then Bonded

**Status:** PROPOSED, 15 Oct 2026. Not in the campaign yet. When the owner approves the Day 0 copy below, it enters `campaign/weeks-01-02.md` as Amendment 2, recorded in `campaign/CHANGELOG.md` the same way as Amendment 1. Days 1 to 14 follow in Stage 2.

**Why.** The owner reviewed the Day 0 proofs on 15 Oct and rejected the messaging, because the slides talk about features. The new direction:
- every post follows one arc
- nothing talks about features
- some slides use a photo as the whole background, with text on it at clear contrast

The decisions are D-2026-10-15-1 to D-2026-10-15-3 in `decisions/log.md`.

## The arc, for every post

1. **The problem.** A specific moment the parent recognizes from their own week. Not a parenting concept.
2. **The feeling.** Why it wears them down. Said plainly, without reassurance or a pep talk.
3. **What to do.** One or two practical things they can try this week, with or without an app.
4. **Bonded, then the waitlist.** It comes once, at the end, and never as a feature list.

**Rules that carry over from the campaign:**
- **Voice:** specific, warm, and funny where something is funny.
- **No invented testimonials,** and never a claim that something happened to a real family.
- **None of the "Never write" list,** and no em dashes or emoji.
- **Family time is never something to earn.**
- **Talk to parents, not children.**

**Rules that are new:**
- **No feature talk.** "A family app for running the week together" (the approved positioning) is the most a post says about what Bonded does.
- **Every post ends with Bonded and the waitlist.** This replaces the old mix of 8 editorial-only posts, 4 natural connections and 2 product demonstrations.
- **The app appears only on the waitlist slide.** It's a real capture from `design/screenshots-manifest.md`, showing the in-app family name ("The Miller Family") with no "Demo family" caption under the phone (D-2026-10-15-3). No copy may say or suggest the Millers are real users.
- **Full-photo slides.**
  - The photo fills the frame. An Ink gradient sits behind the words, and the words are Paper.
  - There's no Hearth on a photo.
  - Every line of text measures at least 4.5:1 against the lightest 5% of the pixels behind it. The renderer checks this on every slide.
  - Paper slides use the approved type layouts.
- **AI photos carry the platform's AI label** when posted: "AI-generated content" on TikTok, "AI info" on Instagram and Facebook.

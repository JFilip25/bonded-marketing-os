## The arc, for every post

1. **The problem.** A real moment from the parent's week. Not a parenting idea.
2. **The feeling.** Why it wears them down. Said plainly, without a pep talk.
3. **What to do.** One to three things they can try this week, with or without an app.
4. **Bonded, then the waitlist.** It comes once, at the end, and never as a feature list.

## Plain words (D-2026-10-15-5)

**Write so an 8-year-old can understand it. Don't write like an 8-year-old.**
- **Short sentences.** Most slides have 4 to 9 words per sentence.
- **Everyday words.** "Tiring", not "exhausting". "Jobs", not "responsibilities".
- **Real examples instead of ideas.** "Lunches. Homework. Whose turn it is to feed the dog." Not "the mental load".
- **Say the literal thing.** No idioms or metaphors that need decoding: not "get the week out of one head", "keeping score" or "on the line".
- **The check:** the Flesch-Kincaid reading grade must be 4 or lower on every slide and 5 or lower on captions, with no word over 3 syllables except names and everyday words like "everything". The scores are recorded below.

## Photos and plain slides (D-2026-10-15-4)

- **Size of a post:** usually 5 to 7 slides.
- **Mix:** about 80% of the slides have a photo as the whole background. Plain slides are about 20%, and never more than 2 in a post.
- **Photo source:** the owner's Drive folder `First_2W`.
  - Each post uses that day's own files (`dNN_c01`, `dNN_c02`, ...) where they fit the words.
  - Other folder files fill the rest (D-2026-10-15-8), with no file used twice.
  - Files that are cleaned-up stock previews, copies of other people's posts, or that show someone else's captions are never used.
- **On a photo:**
  - An Ink gradient sits behind the words, and the words are Paper.
  - There's no Hearth on a photo.
  - Every line of text must measure at least 4.5:1 against the lightest 5% of the pixels behind it. The renderer checks this, and it also checks the photo share of each post.
- **Plain slides** are Paper with the approved type.
- **AI photos carry the platform's AI label** when posted: "AI-generated content" on TikTok, "AI info" on Instagram and Facebook.

## Rules that carry over

- **Voice:** specific, warm, and funny where something is funny. No invented testimonials, and never a claim that something happened to a real family. Nothing from the "Never write" list, and no em dashes or emoji.
- **Family time is never something to earn.** Talk to parents, not children.
- **No feature talk.** The most a post says about what Bonded does is the positioning, in plain words: "an app that helps your whole family plan the week and share the jobs".
- **Every post ends with Bonded and the waitlist** (D-2026-10-15-2).
- **The app appears only on the waitlist slide** (D-2026-10-15-3):
  - It's a real capture from `design/screenshots-manifest.md`.
  - It shows the in-app family name ("The Miller Family") with no "Demo family" caption.
  - No copy may say or suggest the Millers are real users.

# Proposed US localization for campaign weeks 1–2

**Status:** APPLIED on 10 Oct 2026. The owner chose option B, one US-English master, and approved every drafted line (D-2026-10-10-4). Applied as Amendment 1, line by line in `campaign/CHANGELOG.md`, with consistency follow-ons in brief notes only. Three more captions are proposed in `proposals/us-localization-addendum.md`.

**Why.** Tier 1 is the United States. Tier 2 is the UK, English-speaking EU, Australia and New Zealand (`strategy/market-priority.md`). The approved copy is written in UK English.

The campaign file itself flags the choice: "First market. US, UK or EU decides posting times, spelling (allowance or pocket money) and hashtags" (l.901).

## Options for the owner

| Option | What happens | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Keep the approved UK wording everywhere** | Nothing changes | Some friction for US parents. "Football" reads as American football, and "bins", "hoover" and "pocket money" read as foreign. |
| **B. One US-English master for all markets** (recommended) | Each approved line below becomes the single version on art and captions. Tier 2 readers understand US terms. | One creative per post, as the campaign requires ("One file per post goes to all three platforms"). Needs a line-by-line approval. |
| **C. Two versions** | US art and captions, plus the UK originals for Tier 2 | Doubles production and Buffer drafts. Organic accounts can't geo-target posts, so both audiences would see both. Not recommended. |

The product already says **"Allowance"** in its own UI (the add menu, screen 05). Option B also brings the campaign in line with the app.

Option B was chosen. The claim amendments (`proposals/claim-amendments.md`) were applied first, and the US wording on top.
